Japanese Kanji Atlas privacy notice

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: Japanese Kanji Atlas requires no account, shows no advertising, does not track users, and does not send learning progress to a server. Progress is stored on the user’s device.

Data collected

The developer of Japanese Kanji Atlas does not collect personal data, usage analytics, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers through the app. The app has no account or cloud-sync feature.

Information stored on the device

Learning progress, preferences, and review state remain in the app’s local storage on the device. Depending on operating-system behaviour, deleting the app may also delete this information.

Network connections

Lesson content, recorded audio and fonts are bundled with the app. Fonts do not require a connection to an external server. Source and feedback links open only when the user chooses them.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly request or collect personal data from children. It has no accounts, chat, social sharing, or behavioural advertising.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to hello@zeynepkaya.app.
